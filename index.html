Who We Are

We are a group of students and professors from Indiana Tech.

The AI & Cybersecurity Clinic brings together faculty and students who are passionate about helping small businesses make smarter technology decisions. We combine classroom knowledge with practical experience to support businesses that want to use AI responsibly and protect themselves online.

What We Offer

We help small businesses with AI integration and cyber security for free.

01

AI Integration

We help businesses identify where AI can save time, improve customer service, and support daily operations in a practical way.

02

Cybersecurity Guidance

We review common risks, recommend simple protections, and help businesses build safer digital habits without overwhelming their team.

03

Workflow Support

We show businesses how to use AI tools in ways that support operations, communication, and customer experience more effectively.

04

Free Consultation

We offer no-cost guidance to help small businesses get started, ask questions, and take the next steps with confidence.
